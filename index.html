Personal Gmail Reader

Personal Gmail Reader is a small private script that its owner runs on their own Mac to search and read messages in their own Gmail account, for example to find a purchase receipt while doing household bookkeeping.

It is not a public service. It has a single user, its owner, and nobody else can sign in to it.

  • It asks Google only for read-only access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • Everything runs locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
